# Prioritization & Roadmapping, Module 2 Lab

## Prioritize your Rocks and your Hard Nos
- **Rock #1:** Item 2: Photo markup and annotation for field teams.
Help foremen show exactly where a problem is and what needs attention, reducing follow-up explanations across texts and office reports. Directly supports KR3: reduce duplicate reporting; may support KR1 when unclear information delays resolution.
- **Rock #2:** Item 4: Simplify the daily log from 14 required fields to 4.
Reduce the reporting burden explicitly identified by foremen, making Meridian easier to use repeatedly during actual work. Supports KR2: increase retained field usage. Preserve required enterprise information through existing project data or office-owned steps, subject to validation.
- **Rock #3:** Item 11: Push notifications for schedule changes.
Give foremen relevant changes affecting their crew without requiring them to search the platform or chase updates. Supports KR2: increase retained field usage by providing a practical reason to return. Notifications must be targeted and based on current schedules.
- **❌ Hard No #1:** Item 10: Executive reporting dashboard with custom KPIs.
We will defer this dashboard despite two at-risk renewals because it would divert capacity toward executive reporting before proving field value; leadership must explicitly review and accept the renewal exposure.
- **❌ Hard No #2:** Item 12: Time-tracking module.
We will not add a new administrative workflow to support three contract negotiations while our priority is reducing the effort required for foremen to use Meridian.
- **❌ Hard No #3:** Item 5: Automated compliance checklist generator.
We will preserve existing compliance functionality but defer new automation because the request from three enterprise clients does not yet demonstrate an improvement in field adoption or blocker resolution.
